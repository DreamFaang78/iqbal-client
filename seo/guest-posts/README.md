# Guest Post Drafts — Manual Outreach Pack

These are ready-to-pitch, original articles for **real editorial placements** on
health/wellness blogs, women's health publications, and local Kanpur/UP lifestyle sites.
Each one targets one of the priority keywords and includes one natural link back to
a relevant page on hommed.org.

## How to use these

1. **Find real sites** in the health, women's wellness, dermatology, or local-Kanpur
   niche that accept guest contributions (search `"write for us" homeopathy` /
   `"guest post" women's health India` / Kanpur lifestyle blogs, local news portals).
2. **Pitch first** — send the site a 2-3 line email with the headline + a one-paragraph
   summary, and ask if they'd like the full draft. Don't mass-submit the same article
   to many sites (duplicate content gets rejected and can look spammy).
3. **Tailor before sending** — tweak the intro/examples to fit the publication's tone
   and audience. Keep the link, but let the editor adjust anchor text if they prefer.
4. **Track placements** in a simple sheet: site, DA, publish date, URL, target keyword,
   anchor text. Check indexing status after 1-2 weeks (`site:` search or GSC).

## What NOT to do

- Don't post these via bulk/automated logins to Web 2.0 accounts (Medium, Substack,
  etc.) — mass-publishing the same article across many low-effort accounts is a link
  scheme under Google's spam policies and risks a manual action against hommed.org,
  which is especially damaging for a medical (YMYL) site.
- Don't submit the exact same article text to multiple sites — duplicate guest content
  gets flagged by editors and by Google.
- Keep anchor text natural ("Dr. Iqbal's homeopathic clinic in Kanpur" rather than
  exact-match "best homeopathic doctor in kanpur" every time).
